About Me

I am a Computer Science student at University Academy 92 Manchester, studying BSc (Hons) Computer Science. I am currently developing my foundations in programming, problem solving and software development.

My academic journey started in Health and Social Care, where I completed a Level 3 Extended Diploma. During this time, I completed 175 hours of placement experience in a nursery school over two years. Although I valued the experience I gained, I realised that I wanted to pursue a different career and became increasingly interested in technology and programming.

I am particularly interested in software development and artificial intelligence and machine learning. I am still exploring the different areas of Computer Science and looking forward to discovering which areas interest me most throughout my degree.

During my first year, my goals are to become a stronger programmer, build several meaningful projects and develop my understanding of data structures and algorithms. I am also working towards securing a software engineering or technology internship for summer 2027.
